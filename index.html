xboom Frequently Asked Questions

What makes LG xboom by will.i.am speakers different?
LG xboom by will.i.am combines LG audio technology with sound tuned by will.i.am. The lineup includes portable Bluetooth speakers, party speakers, and earbuds designed for everyday listening, outdoor use, travel, karaoke, and larger gatherings.
Which LG xboom speaker is right for me?
Choose a speaker based on where and how you plan to use it. The LG xboom Mini offers a compact design for everyday portability, while the LG xboom Rock is made for more rugged outdoor use. For parties and karaoke, the LG xboom Stage 501 provides more powerful sound and performance-focused features.
Are LG xboom speakers waterproof?
Water- and dust-resistance ratings vary by model. The xboom Mini and xboom Rock are IP67 rated, while the xboom Stage 501 has an IPX4 water-resistance rating. Review the specifications for each model before using it near water.
Does the LG xboom Stage 501 support karaoke?
Yes. The LG xboom Stage 501 is designed for karaoke and live performances. It includes AI Karaoke, microphone connectivity, a built-in smartphone stand, and multiple speaker orientations for flexible party setups.
Can I use LG xboom speakers outdoors?
Yes. Several LG portable Bluetooth speakers are designed for outdoor listening with portable builds, long battery life, and water or dust-resistant construction. The xboom Rock is especially suited to travel and outdoor activities because of its rugged design and IP67 rating.
